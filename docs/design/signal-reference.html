Signal

Light theme only. Colour works in two layers. Strong colour is a signal: something you can do, something that needs you, or something on track. Soft pastel colour is identity: each bucket, account group and kind of decision keeps its own hue everywhere, so you can find Groceries by colour before you read it.

Every page uses the same dense pattern: a header row, one summary strip with the number that matters and a small illustration, then a main list beside a side panel. Rows are grouped inside one card with hairlines, not boxed one by one.

Iris: you can do thisMain buttons, selection, progress, links
Tomato: this needs youInbox count, over budget, old imports, lowest point
Green: this is on trackPaid, on track, net worth went up
Pastels: identityBucket and account tiles, their bars, decision types. Always soft fill, deep glyph
Page tintEach page tints its summary strip: Inbox peach, Accounts butter, Buckets mint, Plan lavender
Everything else is neutralText, borders, group labels, navigation
